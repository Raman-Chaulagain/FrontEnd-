For the wonder of it all.

We’re aquarists first. A shop second. And endlessly fascinated by the world beneath the surface.

IT STARTED WITH ONE TANK.

Some things pull you a little deeper.

For us, it was a single freshwater tank, a handful of aquatic plants, and the first flash of color between green leaves. What began as a personal hobby in Hetauda grew into a shared passion: helping more people experience that quiet moment of wonder.

We established Frn's Aquarium not just to sell products, but to curate what we trust in our own aquariums. Healthy fish, robust plants, dependable equipment, and genuine advice from fellow water keepers.

A small shop. A whole other world.

THE THINGS WE WON'T COMPROMISE

Care is the whole point.

Life comes first.

We source thoughtfully, care for new arrivals with diligence, and ensure every fish and plant is healthy before it reaches your tank. Living things deserve patience.

Choose well. Keep longer.

We favor dependable tools, healthy plants, and practical equipment that stands the test of time, giving your aquatic ecosystem the best start possible.

Knowledge belongs to everyone.

No gatekeeping. No question too small. Whether it’s your first water change or your next aquascape, you deserve clear, honest guidance from keepers who care.
