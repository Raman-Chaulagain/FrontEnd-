A LITTLE CLARITY

Before you dive in.

The answers to a few things our fellow keepers ask most often.

All questions Shipping & arrival Returns & orders Care & compatibility

How do you ship fish and plants safely?

Fish travel in insulated packaging with oxygen and temperature control packs when needed. Livestock and fragile plants are insulated and packed fresh for delivery across Nepal.

What is your live-arrival guarantee?

We guarantee healthy arrival. If a fish or plant arrives in poor condition, WhatsApp or email clear photos within two hours of delivery for a quick resolution.

When will my order arrive?

Orders in Hetauda are often delivered same-day or next-day. Delivery to other cities across Nepal takes 2–4 business days via express courier.

Can I return equipment or an aquarium?

Unused dry goods in original packaging can be returned within 7 days. Contact us first for instructions. Live fish and tissue plants cannot be returned once delivered.

How do I know which fish are right for my tank?

Tell us your tank volume, water filter setup, and current residents. We’ll help you choose compatible species and sensible stocking levels.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible via phone or WhatsApp (+977 9864776061). We can make changes before your order leaves our shop.
